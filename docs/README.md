# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme manages cross-functional projects with a customer-first, iterative approach: teams deliver small, testable increments, use evidence to measure outcomes, and improve as they learn. Clear ownership supports delivery: the Project Manager (PM) coordinates plans, risks, and communication, while the Product Manager (PdM) and Product Lead guide outcomes, priorities, and stakeholder alignment.

## Project management process

Projects move through a connected lifecycle:

1. **Initiation:** Validate the business need, define measurable success, identify stakeholders, and decide whether to proceed.
2. **Planning:** Turn approved work into a prioritized backlog, define acceptance criteria and the Definition of Done, estimate capacity, and map milestones, risks, and dependencies.
3. **Execution and tracking:** Deliver and review work in small increments, track progress on the project board, maintain quality through testing and code review, and raise blockers through the agreed escalation path.
4. **Release:** Meet acceptance and CI requirements, prepare release notes and rollback plans, deploy, verify the release, and communicate outcomes.
5. **Retrospective and improvement:** Capture what went well and what can improve, assign follow-up actions, and use their results to refine how the team works.

Regular team and stakeholder communication, active risk management, and outcome metrics support every phase.
